✨ Live preview above — here's how to play

  • 👆 Tap any arrow to send it flying off the board.
  • ❤️ Tap one that's blocked and it shakes red — that costs a heart.
  • ⏱️ Clear the whole board before the timer hits zero.
  • 🔥 Fire arrows burn every arrow touching them when cleared — instant bonus points.
  • ❄️ Frozen arrows won't budge until every arrow on one specific side of them is gone — then they thaw.
  • 🔒 Locked arrows need their hidden "key" arrow cleared first before they'll move.
  • 💡 Stuck? Spend a hint to reveal a move that's ready to go.
  • ↩️ One free undo per level if you tap the wrong one.
  • ⭐ Clear a level without losing a heart for a perfect 3-star run.
